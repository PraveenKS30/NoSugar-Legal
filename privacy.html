Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 9, 2026  ·  Last updated: May 13, 2026

Short version: No Sugar stores your habit data on your device. If you choose to create an account, it is synced to a secure cloud database. We never sell your data, show you ads, or share your personal information with third parties.

1. Who we are

No Sugar ("the app", "we", "us") is an independent mobile application that helps you track sugar-free days and build healthier habits. The app is developed and operated by Praveen Kumar S.

Contact: c2praveen30jun@gmail.com

2. What data we collect

We collect only what is necessary to make the app work.

Data you provide directly
  • Display name — optional, used only for personalized greetings inside the app
  • Mascot name — optional nickname you give your in-app companion
  • Goal & motivations — free-text fields to remind you why you started
  • Email address — only if you choose to create a named account to back up your data
  • Password — stored as a cryptographic hash by our auth provider; we never see it in plaintext
Data generated by your activity
  • Daily check-ins — whether each day was clean or a slip
  • Slip logs — the category you select (e.g. "dessert", "soda") and an optional free-text note
  • Mood field — optional mood rating on check-in
  • Craving journal entries — free-text notes you write in the SOS toolkit
  • Reminder preference — the time you choose for your daily notification
  • Analytics preference — whether you choose to share anonymous product analytics
  • Theme preference — light or dark mode (stored on-device only)
Data we do NOT collect
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, or camera (unless you explicitly use the photo-wins feature in a future update)
  • Device identifiers (IDFA, GAID)
  • Browsing history
  • Health data from HealthKit or Google Fit

3. How we store your data

Default — offline only: All data is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage (React Native's encrypted local database). It never leaves your device unless you create an account.

If you create an account: Your data is synced to a Supabase database hosted on AWS infrastructure. Each user's data is isolated by Row-Level Security policies — no other user can read your rows, and neither can we without direct database access (which we do not perform routinely).

Data at rest is encrypted by the cloud provider. Data in transit is encrypted via TLS 1.2+.

4. How we use your data

  • To show you your streak, calendar, and statistics inside the app
  • To send you daily reminder notifications at your chosen time (processed entirely on-device)
  • To sync your data across your own devices when you are signed in
  • To restore your data if you reinstall the app or switch phones
  • To diagnose crashes and improve app reliability
  • To understand aggregate feature usage only if you enable anonymous analytics in Settings

We do not use your data for advertising, third-party ad targeting, or selling user profiles.

5. Third-party services

The app uses the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy.

  • Supabase (database & authentication) — supabase.com/privacy
  • Expo / EAS (build & over-the-air updates) — expo.dev/privacy
  • Sentry (crash reporting, if configured) — sentry.io/privacy
  • PostHog (anonymous product analytics, only if you opt in) — posthog.com/privacy

We do not integrate advertising SDKs or social login providers. Product analytics is off by default and can be enabled or disabled any time in Settings → Privacy.

6. Your rights — GDPR & CCPA

Regardless of where you live, you have the following rights:

  • Access & portability — export all your data as JSON from Settings → Account → Export my data
  • Deletion — permanently delete your account and all associated data from Settings → Account → Delete account. This is irreversible and triggers a cascading delete across all tables.
  • Correction — edit your name, goal, and motivations at any time in Settings
  • Withdrawal of consent — turn off cloud sync at any time by signing out; the app continues to work offline
  • Analytics opt-out — turn off anonymous analytics any time in Settings → Privacy

If you are in the EU/EEA and wish to exercise a right that is not available in-app, email us at c2praveen30jun@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Data retention

Cloud data is retained for as long as your account is active. Anonymous sessions (no email linked) that have had no activity for 12 months may be purged. You can delete your data at any time.

Local (on-device) data is controlled entirely by you — uninstalling the app removes it.

8. Children's privacy

No Sugar is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Notifications

The app may request permission to send local notifications for daily reminders and milestone alerts. These notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device — no notification payload is routed through our servers. You can disable them at any time in the app's Settings or in your device's system notification settings.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the current version. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
